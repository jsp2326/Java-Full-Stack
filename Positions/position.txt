Position:
---------


        -static : Default Position
        -relative : Element is relative to itself.
        -absolute : positined relative to its closest positined ancestor
        -fixed : Positioned relative to browser.
        -sticky : positined based on user's scroll position


Z-indx:
------

it decides the stack level of elsements
Overlapping elements with a larger z-index cover those with a smaller one.

        z-index: auto(0)
        z-index:1/2/...
        z-index:-1/-2/...
